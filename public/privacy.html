How we use it and our legal basis

  • To provide the app: create and secure your account, save your entries, sync them between devices, calculate targets, streaks and weekly reports. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Health data: we process it only with your explicit consent (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(a)), given when you create an account. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account, which deletes the data.
  • Service emails: account confirmation and password reset. Legal basis: contract.
  • Security and fixing problems: using technical logs to keep the service running and protect it from abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interests.

Reminders are scheduled on your device. They don't send your data anywhere.

Waiting list

If you join the waiting list on our website, we store your email address, the date you joined and, if you arrived through a link with a reference, that reference. We use it only to email you about the launch of DAILY 15 and a founding-member offer, on the basis of your consent. You can withdraw consent and ask us to delete your email at any time by replying to any email from us or contacting [privacy contact email]. We delete the waiting list within [12 months] of launch.

Apple Health

DAILY 15 reads from and writes to Apple Health only if you allow it, and only the data types listed above. You can change or remove access at any time in the Health app. We use Apple Health data only to provide DAILY 15's features. We never use it for advertising or marketing, never sell it or share it with data brokers, and never store it in iCloud.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Database rules ensure each account can read and change only its own data. Passwords are never stored in readable form.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict our use of it, and withdraw consent. In the app you can:

  • export all your data as a file from Profile, Your data, Export;
  • correct any entry by editing it;
  • delete your account and data from Profile, Your data, Delete my account and data.

For anything else, email [privacy contact email]. We'll reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

DAILY 15 is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from younger children.

Not medical advice

DAILY 15 provides general fitness information. Calorie, nutrition and exercise figures are estimates. Speak to a GP before starting a new exercise or diet plan if you have a health condition, are pregnant or are returning from injury.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
