Common questions

I didn't get the confirmation or password reset email

Check your junk folder, then use 'Send it again' in the app. Links work on the device where you asked for them. If nothing arrives within ten minutes, email us.

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, choose 'Forgot password?', enter your email and follow the link. To change it while signed in, go to Profile, Account, Password.

Does my data sync between devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same account and your workouts, food, weigh-ins and activity sync automatically. Changes made on two devices at the same time are merged. Progress photos stay on the device where you took them.

How do I connect Apple Health?

In the iPhone app, go to Profile, Integrations, Connect Apple Health, and choose what DAILY 15 can read. Steps, distance, active energy, exercise minutes, weigh-ins and workouts then sync each time you open the app. To change access later, open the Health app, tap your profile, then Apps, then DAILY 15.

My reminders aren't arriving

Check notifications are allowed for DAILY 15 in the iPhone Settings app, and that each reminder is switched on in Profile, Reminders. In a web browser, reminders appear only while DAILY 15 is open.

Why are some food values marked 'Est.'?

They come from our food list or the Open Food Facts database and may differ from your product. Tap the entry to correct it from the label.

How are my calorie and protein targets worked out?

From your age, height, weight, activity level and goal, using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. For fat loss, the target is at most 500 kcal a day below your estimated needs. They are starting points. Change them any time in Profile, Daily targets, and check them with a GP or registered dietitian if you have a medical condition.

How do I export or delete my data?

Profile, Your data. Export saves everything as a file. 'Delete my account and data' permanently removes your account and everything in it.
